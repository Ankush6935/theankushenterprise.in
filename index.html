01 // ABOUT ANKUSH ENTERPRISES

Sovereign Engineering. Accountable Lifecycle Delivery.

Established in 2018 and headquartered in Secunderabad, Telangana, M/s Ankush Enterprises operates adjacent to major military cantonments, tactical air bases, and national defence research laboratories. We engineer, integrate, and sustain high-assurance computing clusters, C4I command centers, avionics support systems, and perimeter security networks.

By combining Class-0 ESD integration infrastructure and 72-hour thermal validation protocols with direct Tier-1 OEM distribution from Ingram Micro India and Redington Limited, we deliver zero-counterfeit hardware backed by comprehensive multi-year lifecycle SLAs.
