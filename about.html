01 // WHO WE ARE

From Specialized Equipment to Integrated Command Systems.

M/s Ankush Enterprises operates adjacent to major military cantonments, tactical training bases, and national defence research laboratories in Secunderabad, Telangana. Our practice spans custom multi-GPU AI computing, electronic diagnostics, C4I command operations center integration, aviation simulator sustainment, and high-security perimeter surveillance.

With a flawless 100% on-time CRAC acceptance record across multi-command institutional deliveries, we pair rapid local engineering responsiveness with direct Tier-1 OEM hardware provenance through our authorized partnerships with Ingram Micro India and Redington Limited.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system across integration and field service
  • Class-0 ESD assembly facility with 72-hour sustained thermal burn-in validation
  • Statutory EMD exemption under GFR 2017 Rule 170(i) and NSIC Single Point Registration
  • Guaranteed < 4-hour priority on-station engineering response across core operational hubs
